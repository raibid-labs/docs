Theme Selection Guide

By Use Case

For Coding:

  • Dracula (dark)
  • Monokai (dark)
  • One Dark (dark)
  • One Light (light)

For Long Sessions:

  • Solarized Dark/Light (scientifically balanced)
  • Catppuccin Mocha/Latte (soft pastels)
  • Nord (calm, minimal)

For Aesthetics:

  • Tokyo Night (modern, clean)
  • Synthwave (retro, fun)
  • Gruvbox (vintage)

For Daylight:

  • Solarized Light
  • One Light
  • Catppuccin Latte

For Night:

  • Dracula (default)
  • Tokyo Night
  • Nord

By Color Preference

Purple Lovers: Dracula, One Dark Blue Lovers: Nord, Tokyo Night Warm Colors: Monokai, Gruvbox Pastels: Catppuccin Neon/Vibrant: Synthwave Balanced: Solarized
